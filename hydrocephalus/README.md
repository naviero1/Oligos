# Hydrocephalus

Per-measurement toxicity data for therapeutic oligonucleotides against
**hydrocephalus**, one of the eight toxicities named in the NIH/NCATS
Oligonucleotide Toxicity (OligoTox) Open Data Challenge brief. **147
measurements across 13 oligonucleotides.** Curated as part of a single CNS
corpus and separated into this folder by the partition below. A self-contained
two-table dataset, but it inherits its schema, methodology, verification record
and source registry from [`../chronic-neurotoxicity/`](../chronic-neurotoxicity/).

## Derivation

The CNS curation was carried out as **one corpus of 2,540 measurements** serving
two named Challenge endpoints. Every row declares which one it serves through
`challenge_priority`, so the partition is read off the data rather than inferred:
`high_hydrocephalus` (147) here, everything else (2,393) to
`chronic-neurotoxicity/`. The split is **disjoint and exhaustive** — no
`measurement_id` appears on both sides, and 147 + 2,393 = 2,540, verified in this
pass by set comparison of the two `measurements.csv` files. It is regenerated by
[`../_shared/scripts/split_cns_by_endpoint.py`](../_shared/scripts/split_cns_by_endpoint.py),
which fails loudly if the counts stop summing to the corpus total.

`oligos.csv` is filtered to the 13 oligos this folder's measurements reference.
**5 of the 13 also appear in `chronic-neurotoxicity/data/oligos.csv`** (`CNS012`,
`CNS013`, `CNS014`, `CNS276`, `CNS286`) — not double-counting: an oligo is a
compound identity, not a toxicity observation.

## Data files

| File | Grain | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| `data/oligos.csv` | one row per oligonucleotide (identity + design predictors) | 13 × 17 |
| `data/measurements.csv` | one row per oligo × model × region × delivery × dose × readout | 147 × 26 |
| `data/oligotox_cns_merged.csv` | denormalised analysis view, generated | 147 × 42 |

The normalised tables are the source of truth. The merged view is rebuilt by
`scripts/build_merged_cns.py` and never hand-edited; re-running it in this pass
reproduced the committed file byte-for-byte. Columns, vocabularies and the
`neurotox_grade` 0–3 rubric are in
[`schema-cns.md`](../chronic-neurotoxicity/schema-cns.md), grading discipline in
[`METHODOLOGY-CNS.md`](../chronic-neurotoxicity/METHODOLOGY-CNS.md).

## Distributions

| `study_type` | clinical 133 | animal in vivo 12 | in vitro 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`species`** | human 133 | rat 10 | mouse 3, monkey 1 |
| **`readout_category`** | clinical_neuro_outcome 73 | imaging 58 | functional 14, histopathology 2 |
| **`neurotox_grade`** | 0 → 63 | 1 → 14 | 2 → 54, 3 → 16 |

`delivery_method`: intrathecal 115, `TBD` 18, intracerebroventricular 11, gymnotic
free uptake 2, systemic 1. `endpoint_domain` is `hydrocephalus` on 143 rows and
`clinical_neuro_ae` on 4; all 147 carry `challenge_priority = high_hydrocephalus`.

**This endpoint is overwhelmingly clinical: 133 of 147 rows are human clinical
observations**, led by clinical-outcome and imaging readouts (131 of 147 between
them). Only 12 rows are animal in vivo and 2 in vitro, so the mechanistic floor is
thin and must not be overstated — the one animal study that looks like an
exception is read under *Honest limitations* in
[`README-CNS.md`](../chronic-neurotoxicity/README-CNS.md).

**16 rows are grade 3**, the rubric's "hydrocephalus requiring intervention" band,
on four molecules: nusinersen (6), tominersen (5), valeriasen (4), and the Gnai2
antisense oligodeoxynucleotide (1 — the only non-human grade-3 row). The 63
grade-0 rows are deliberate, including a no-oligonucleotide-exposure comparator
(`CNS591`, 7 rows).

## Sources

The 147 rows cite 44 `source_id` values resolving to 40 distinct documents — EMA
and MHRA regulatory material, ClinicalTrials.gov adverse-event modules, FDA
reviews, conference material and primary literature. Retrieved documents live in
[`../_shared/sources/cns/`](../_shared/sources/cns/) (45 files plus an `fpo/` subdirectory of 16 patent HTML captures, 61 in all; shared with
`chronic-neurotoxicity/`). The registry,
[`SOURCES-CNS.md`](../chronic-neurotoxicity/SOURCES-CNS.md), is keyed by
`source_ref` and all 40 resolve there; its row counts are **corpus-wide (2,540
rows), not scoped to this folder** — it predates the split.

`redistribution` across the 147 rows: `public_domain` 78, `summary_stat` 34,
`cc_by` 22, `verify` 13 — the `verify` rows have unresolved rights and must be
settled before public release.

## Open work

- **Sequences.** 6 of 13 oligos carry a published sequence; the other 7 are `TBD`
  because no redistribution-permitted document giving one was retrieved.
- **No folder-local schema.** This folder inherits `schema-cns.md` from a sibling
  endpoint folder. Either it carries its own copy or the shared schema moves
  somewhere neutral; as it stands the link will rot.
- **QC runs clean.** `scripts/qc_cns.py` and `scripts/build_merged_cns.py` were both
  repointed from the pre-partition filenames (`data/cns_oligos.csv`,
  `data/cns_measurements.csv`) to this folder's `data/oligos.csv` and
  `data/measurements.csv`. `python scripts/qc_cns.py` reports 0 errors, 0 warnings.
- **Duplicate source identifiers.** Four ClinicalTrials.gov documents carry two
  `source_id` values each (`CNSSRC_CTG_NCT03070119`/`CT_NCT03070119`, same pairing
  for `NCT03342053`, `NCT03761849`, `NCT04089566`) — which is why 44 ids resolve to
  40 documents. They should be collapsed to one id per document.
- **Grades are provisional** pending subject-matter review, as across the whole
  CNS corpus. See [`VERIFICATION-CNS.md`](../chronic-neurotoxicity/VERIFICATION-CNS.md)
  and [`NEXT-STEPS-CNS.md`](../chronic-neurotoxicity/NEXT-STEPS-CNS.md).

Repository front door and endpoint register: [`../README.md`](../README.md).
