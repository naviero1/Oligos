# OligoTox-Kidney — Methodology Document

**NIH/NCATS OligoTox Open Data Challenge, Phase 2 · Endpoint: kidney toxicity**

*Phase 2 methodology deliverable. Target length ≤ 5 pages when rendered to PDF. The
long-form internal documentation is `METHODOLOGY.md`; this is the submission version.*

---

## 1. Nature of the dataset, and what "materials and methods" means here

OligoTox-Kidney is an **in-silico curation** of pre-existing, publicly reported
nephrotoxicity data. No wet-lab experiment was performed. The materials are therefore
**documents**, and the methods are **source identification, extraction, harmonisation,
grading, provenance capture and quality control** — i.e. how the dataset was assembled and
computationally processed.

We state this plainly at the top because it determines what can and cannot be reported
below. In particular it is why §5 reports oligo *identity* characterisation in full and
oligo *purity* as absent.

**Scope control.** The dataset is strict-kidney. This is verified rather than asserted:
all 246 rows carry `is_kidney_specific = TRUE`; `tissue` takes only `kidney` (79) and
`proximal_tubule` (167); and an automated audit confirms zero hepatic readouts, zero
hepatic system models and zero hepatotoxicity sources cited. Hepatotoxicity source
material held elsewhere in the repository contributes **no rows** to this dataset.

## 3. Methods — data model

Two normalised UTF-8 CSV tables joined on `oligo_id`:

- **`oligos.csv`** (65 × 20) — one row per unique oligonucleotide: identity, sequence,
  chemistry and design predictors.
- **`measurements.csv`** (246 × 25) — one row per **oligo × system/subject × delivery ×
  concentration/dose × readout**. One oligo at one concentration read on two biomarkers is
  two rows.

A denormalised analysis view (`oligotox_kidney_merged.csv`, 246 × 44) is **generated by
script and never hand-edited**; the two normalised tables remain the source of truth. A
derived `human_animal_bridge.csv` (15 oligos) materialises the compounds carrying evidence
on both sides of the human/animal divide.

Missing values are the literal string `TBD` — never guessed, never imputed as zero. The
full data dictionary, controlled vocabularies and grading rubric are in `schema.md`.

## 4. Methods — extraction

Every transformation is a committed, re-runnable script. No derived value is hand-entered.

### 4.1 Full-text extraction from primary sources

PDFs were parsed with layout-preserving text extraction (`pdfplumber`). Per-measurement
values, doses, sequences and exact loci were transcribed into the schema with the source
table or figure recorded in `source_table`.

**Two hazards required specific countermeasures**, because both produce plausible wrong
answers rather than obvious failures:

- **Column interleaving.** The patent table pages extract, under naive text extraction, as
  a flat number stream that cannot be mapped back to (compound, concentration, system,
  timepoint). Both table extractors therefore parse the *layout-preserving* rendering and
  **validate parsed cells against anchor values read from the printed table** before
  writing anything; each refuses to write a partial table if the cell count is wrong.
- **Case-encoded chemistry.** In gapmer sequences, upper case denotes 2′-MOE/cEt wings and
  lower case the DNA gap. Sequence validators must be case-insensitive; a case-sensitive
  check reports these correct rows as malformed.

### 4.2 WHO INN nomenclature derivation

For INN-named oligonucleotides, WHO publishes the complete residue-by-residue chemical
nomenclature (`2'-O-methyl-P-thiocytidylyl-(3'→5')-…`). Sequence is therefore recoverable
by **deterministic parse** rather than transcription. This supplied 20 sequences.

Two properties make it safer than reading a sequence off a table, and both were enforced:

1. **Direction is explicit and asymmetric.** INN writes one strand with (3′→5′) linkages
   (listed 5′→3′) and its partner with (5′→3′) linkages (listed 3′→5′, hence reversed on
   output). Mishandling this yields a silently *reversed* strand. The parser was validated
   by reproducing two sequences already present in the table, character-for-character,
   before any new row was written.
2. **Results are self-checking.** Every duplex was required to show its guide strand as the
   exact reverse complement of its sense strand (ignoring 3′ overhangs), and residue counts
   were cross-checked against the published molecular formula wherever the phosphorus count
   fixes the length (e.g. viltolarsen `P20` → 21 nt; alicaforsen `P19S19` → 20 nt).

### 4.3 Regulatory label retrieval

FDA prescribing information was read via **DailyMed** structured product labels. Renal
content was extracted from the Warnings and Precautions, Adverse Reactions and Use in
Specific Populations sections, recording the section number in `source_table`.

## 5. Methods used to purify and characterise oligo identity

The Phase 2 announcement asks specifically for this. For a curation-type dataset the two
halves have different answers, and we report them separately rather than collapsing them.

### 5.1 Purity — absent, and verified absent

`purity_pct` and `purity_method` are **`TBD` for all 65 oligonucleotides.**

This was established, not assumed. Both source patents were searched programmatically for
purity, HPLC, UPLC, LC-MS and mass-spectrometry language; **neither reports any** (the only
"characterised" matches describe chemical structure, not oligo purity). Regulatory labels
and trial publications do not publish per-batch analytical purity either.

No wet-lab work was performed, so no purity determination could be made independently.
This is a genuine limitation of the curation approach and cannot be closed by further
extraction. It is recorded as an explicit `TBD` column with the reason in `notes`, rather
than left as an unexplained blank.

### 5.2 Identity — established for 55 of 65, with the method recorded per oligo

`identity_confirmation` records **how** each oligo's identity was established:

| Method | n | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| `patent_sequence_listing` | 25 | Formal SEQUENCE LISTING of a US patent, validated base-for-base against the listing rather than the examples table |
| `who_inn_chemical_nomenclature` | 20 | Deterministic parse of WHO INN nomenclature, reverse-complement and molecular-formula checked (§4.2) |
| `regulatory_label` | 7 | Sequence published in an FDA/EMA label |
| `peer_reviewed_publication` | 3 | Sequence printed in a primary publication |
| `not_established` | 10 | Sequence itself is `TBD`; identity unconfirmed |

The 10 unconfirmed are structural, not clerical: 6 proprietary research compounds whose
sponsors never published sequences, 2 class-level aggregate rows for which a single
sequence is not meaningful, and 2 development-code compounds that never received an INN.

Where a sequence is present, the **location of chemical modifications** is carried by
`sugar_modifications` (all 65), `gapmer_design` (wing–gap–wing motif, 40), `ps_count`
(phosphorothioate linkage count, 38) and, for gapmers, position-resolved case encoding
within `sequence_5to3` itself.

## 6. Grading, quality control and known limitations

### 6.1 Grading

Each measurement carries an ordinal `nephrotox_grade` (0–3) assigned from the reported
endpoint per the rubric in `schema.md`: 0 no signal; 1 mild/functional/reversible without
viability loss; 2 moderate injury-biomarker or histopathology; 3 severe AKI,
glomerulonephritis or dose-limiting toxicity.

For the two continuous biomarker panels, grades were thresholded, and **the thresholds are
anchored rather than chosen freely**: in each case the floor is set so the source's own
designated innocuous reference compound grades 0 on every one of its cells. After
application, grade tracks the source's own in-vivo toxicity classification monotonically.
Both rubrics are stated in full in the extraction scripts and in `schema.md`.

The two patent panels use **different normalisations** — % of saline versus % of the
innocuous reference compound — recorded in `readout_unit` so they are never pooled.

All grades carry `grade_provisional` pending subject-matter sign-off.

### 6.2 Quality control

Automated checks run after every ingestion round: schema conformance against the `schema.md`
enums; referential integrity (`measurements.oligo_id` → `oligos.oligo_id`, 0 orphans); no
duplicate primary keys; grades in {0,1,2,3}; and — added after the finding below —
**duplex self-consistency**, requiring each siRNA guide strand to be the exact reverse
complement of its sense strand.

**A corrected error, disclosed.** Final QC found 21 rows (19% of the then-dataset)
recording a rat study as a mouse study, with a published dose left as `TBD`. The source
patent binds its Table 1 to a method section naming "Wistar Han Crl : WI (Han) male rats",
dosing "at 40 mg/kg on days 1 and 8", a "Multiplex MAP **Rat** Kidney Toxicity Magnetic
Bead Panel 2", and sacrifice "on day 15". All four statements were verified against the
patent before correction. Species distribution moved from mouse 30 / rat 8 to rat 29 /
mouse 9.

**A provenance/outcome confound, quantified and mitigated.** Clinical rows cross-tabulated
by sourcing quality against outcome showed provenance predicting outcome almost perfectly
— 0 of 20 search-derived rows reached grade ≥2 against 11 of 22 anchor-sourced rows,
one-sided Fisher p = 4.5 × 10⁻⁵. Direct retrieval of 7 unverified absence claims left only
one standing as a measured negative. Two mitigations were applied: three approved drugs
with *measured* human negatives were added (weakening the association 3.7× to
p = 1.65 × 10⁻⁴), and the schema now carries **`renal_endpoints_measured`**, separating
"measured and unremarkable" from "never looked". 13 grade-0 clinical rows are explicitly
flagged as not supported as measured negatives. Full analysis in `CLINICAL_VALIDATION.md`.

### 6.3 Limitations carried into the release

- Grades are provisional pending scientific sign-off.
- Purity is absent for all 65 oligos (§5.1).
- 36 rows remain search-derived; 13 grade-0 clinical rows are flagged unsupported.
- The confound is reduced, not eliminated.
- 39 of 246 rows lack a numeric dose, mostly awaiting FDA Pharmacology/Toxicology reviews.
- The human/animal bridge is 15 oligos — sufficient for descriptive translation analysis,
  not for a fitted species-correction model.

## 7. Reproducibility

Every derived artefact is regenerable from the two canonical tables by committed scripts:

```
scripts/fill_inn_sequences.py          sequences from WHO INN nomenclature
scripts/extract_patent_table2.py       human in-vitro EGF panel  (US 11,105,794 Table 2)
scripts/extract_n4_table5.py           rat in-vitro EGFR/KIM-1   (US 11,479,818 Table 5)
scripts/fix_patent_species.py          the rat/mouse correction  (§6.2)
scripts/add_identity_characterization.py   identity + purity fields
scripts/add_endpoint_provenance.py     renal_endpoints_measured
scripts/split_human_animal.py          subject_class + bridge view
scripts/build_merged.py                the denormalised analysis view
```

All source PDFs cited above are included in the repository under `sources/` so that any
extracted value can be checked against its origin without re-acquiring the document.
