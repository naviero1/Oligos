## 1. The grain question, answered first because it was asked first

**This endpoint is NOT built one-row-per-oligo.** `data/measurements.csv` is at
experiment-condition grain — one row per oligo × system × delivery × dose × timepoint ×
readout — and it is already the two-table architecture §B requires: a canonical oligo table
(`data/oligos.csv`, 218 rows, identity and chemistry) linked to an experimental-observation
table (2,685 rows), with per-position chemistry in a third (`data/modifications.csv`, 1,039
rows, one row per nucleotide position).

**Nothing has been re-grained and nothing will be.** Where §B's unit is not fully reached, it
is because axes of it are **absent as fields**, not because rows were collapsed:

| §B axis | state here |
|---|---|
| oligo | `oligo_id` → canonical oligo table |
| chemistry | per position in `modifications.csv` |
| **strand state** | **absent.** A duplex is one row with one sequence field; the complement is unrepresentable, not `NOT_REPORTED` |
| dose | `dose_value` + `dose_unit` |
| time | `timepoint` + `exposure_duration` (free text, mixed units) |
| **donor / cell system** | cell system yes (`system_model`, `matrix`); **donor absent** — donor-level and technical replication cannot be distinguished |
| delivery condition | partial: `delivery_method` mixes route and vehicle; **`anticoagulant` absent**, which bites this endpoint specifically |
| endpoint | `readout_name` + `readout_category` |

Full field-by-field audit against §C and §G:
[`research/2026-10-03/schema_coverage_vs_SCIENTIFIC_RULES.csv`](research/2026-10-03/schema_coverage_vs_SCIENTIFIC_RULES.csv),
regenerated by `scripts/audit_scientific_rules.py`. Of 35 named fields: **4 present, 12
present under another name, 7 partial, 12 absent.**

The twelve absent: `strand_role`, `duplex_partner_id`, `endotoxin_level`, `anticoagulant`,

## 2. Rules that change this endpoint's work

Five do. Three are violations, measured.

### 2.1 §E — the CTCAE prohibition. This is the big one.

> "Do not map in-vitro fold-change bins to clinical severity grades (e.g. CTCAE) unless
> clinically validated. Call it experimental response severity."

`coag_tox_grade` assigns CTCAE v5.0 cut-offs to control-referenced ratios. Measured:

| graded rows | 918 of 2,685 |
|---|---:|
| **not clinical — the prohibited case** | **834 (91% of graded)** |
| — animal in vivo | 679 |
| — in vitro | 102 |
| — ex vivo plasma | 50 |
| clinical, where CTCAE is defined | 84 |

**91% of the grades in this dataset are the thing §E prohibits.** The dataset already says
`is_validated_clinical_grade = FALSE` on every row and `schema.md` already opens by calling
the column "a curator-derived research score, not a clinical grade" — so the *claim* was
hedged — but the **derivation** is still CTCAE and the **name** still says toxicity grade.
Hedged wording does not satisfy a prohibition on the mapping itself.

**Not fixed here. German's call**, two decisions: (a) whether non-clinical rows keep an
ordinal at all or are rescoped to `experimental_response_severity`; (b) whether the 84
clinical rows may keep a CTCAE-derived grade given that the denominator is a control mean
rather than CTCAE's upper limit of normal — a deviation this release has always disclosed.
Until he rules, this receipt records that the column is non-compliant.

### 2.2 §E — "do not collapse distinct outcomes"

19 rows carry a composite readout: `coagulopathy_composite`, `aPTT_PT_TT_composite`,
`platelet_count_fibrinogen_PT_INR_composite`, `clinically_relevant_bleeding_composite` and
others. **The sources constructed these, not us** — which §E permits as source reporting, but
they are currently filed under `clotting_time`, `bleeding_outcome` and
`platelet_coag_crosstalk` as if they were single-endpoint observations. One of them
(`platelet_count_fibrinogen_PT_INR_composite`) mixes platelet count with coagulation, the
exact conflation §E names.

Proposed: an `is_composite_endpoint` flag plus the constituent endpoints, so a composite can
be excluded from single-endpoint analysis. **Schema — gated on the Tier 0 crosswalk.** Not added.

### 2.3 §F — the Characterization Gap Register did not exist

> "The field value and the register are both required: the value states the truth, the
> register discharges the requirement."

This endpoint had the values and not the register. **Built:**
[`data/characterization_gap_register.csv`](data/characterization_gap_register.csv) —
**1,055 gap records over all 218 compounds**, one per compound × missing field, each stating
what is missing, why, what was attempted and what would close it, derived from the compound's
own state so it cannot drift from the data.

| missing field | records |
|---|---:|
| `endotoxin_level` | 218 (all) |
| `purity_pct` | 216 |
| `identity_confirmation` | 186 |
| `purity_method` | 172 |
| position-resolved chemistry | 166 |
| printed sequence | 97 |

By lane: human in vitro 441, animal/other 440, human participant 174.

**Two findings inside it.** Ten of the purity gaps are a **withheld limit, not an absent
value** — the regulatory document names the test and redacts the number, so the value exists
and is not public. And `endotoxin_level` is absent for all 218 while endotoxin limits
**appear in EPAR quality sections already held**: that one is recoverable with no new
acquisition. A QC check now fails the build if any missing characterisation field lacks a
register row, so the register cannot fall behind the values again.

### 2.4 §G — grouping columns that "must exist from the start" do not exist

`sequence_family_group` and `paper_group` are absent. Consequences, stated rather than
softened:

- **`paper_group` is recoverable today without a new column.** Every row carries exactly one
  `source_id`, so leave-one-paper-out is computable by grouping on it. §G's LOPO requirement
  is satisfiable now.
- **`sequence_family_group` is not, and must not be faked.** §G also forbids merging
  chemically distinct administered constructs, and this corpus contains the exact trap: a
  GalNAc-conjugated compound sharing a base sequence with its unconjugated parent, and
  `COG-OLG142`, recorded as "same base sequence as BAY 2306001 / ISIS 416858". Sequence
  identity is not construct identity. A candidate grouping can be computed into staging; the
  adjudication is German's.
- Random row splitting is prohibited and no split is published in this release, so nothing
  here claims performance.

### 2.5 §D — no evidence tiers exist

No GOLD/SILVER/BRONZE scoring. `evidence_class` answers a different question (what *kind* of
observation a row is) and is explicitly not a confidence score. Lane separation §D requires
**is** implemented: `species_class`, `human_system` and `human_system_subtype` keep human
clinical, human laboratory and animal apart, and the workbook splits on them.

### 2.6 Rules already satisfied, with the evidence

- **§E "unreported is not negative"** — `NOT_REPORTED` is never a null; 640 measured nulls
  were stratum-tested by four independent reviewers specifically because the sibling kidney
  dataset's negative class was found to be "nobody looked".
- **§E "intended pharmacology is not toxicity"** — two separate booleans; 1,790 of 2,685 rows
  are on-target pharmacology and are labelled as such.
- **§E "keep author result and curator label separate"** — `source_stated_grade` and
  `severity_stated_by_source` are distinct from `coag_tox_grade`; `grade_authority` names who
  assigned it; only 24 rows carry a source-reported grade.
- **§F "never substitute a group-level value for a per-batch one"** — tofersen reads 90% for
  lot TA666853-008 and 94% for TA666853-001, so it carries **no single** `purity_pct`.
- **§I known source corrections** — checked against all 100 sources: Peacock_2009,
  "Hornung 2005"/Herzner, Riera-Tur, Fucini and Lenert are **none of them in this corpus**.
  No action needed, and no re-derivation attempted.
- **§H** — nothing in this release trains or claims a model; the narrative describes intended
  use only.

### 8.1 The correction this forces on my own figures

**Correction accepted (Beebop, 2026-10-03): "11 human-participant compounds with sequence plus
chemistry" is not 11 qualified records.** It is a **compound-grain** count and it tests only
part of gates 2 and 3. Stated correctly:

- **1,219 of 2,685 rows** pass the four gates that are *testable from the tables today*
  (1, 2-sequence-present, 3, 4-context) — over 46 compounds.
- Of those, **184 are human-participant rows** and **0 are strict human-laboratory rows**.
- **No row in this dataset passes all twelve gates**, because gates 2, 4 and 6 fail on columns
  that do not exist, and gate 8 fails on 19 rows. A qualified-record count is therefore **zero**
  under the full contract, and the 1,219 figure must always be reported as *four of twelve
  gates*, never as "qualified".

That is a worse answer than the one I gave, and it is the right one. The earlier framing invited
a reader to treat a compound-level chemistry count as a record-level qualification.
