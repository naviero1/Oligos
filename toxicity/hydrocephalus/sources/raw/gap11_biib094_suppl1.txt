Supplementary data: Preclinical study in cynomolgus macaques 
2 
Results  
3 
Safety  
4 
In total, 11 doses of intrathecal (IT) BIIB094 12 mg, 24 mg or 40 mg were administered to cynomolgus 
5 
macaques (N = 32) once every 4 weeks over 41 weeks. BIIB094 was well tolerated and no adverse effects 
6 
were observed in macaques receiving BIIB094 40 mg. Transient changes in clinical signs were observed 
7 
with the 12 mg and 40 mg doses (mainly in two animals), as well as partially reversible cerebrospinal fluid 
8 
(CSF) cell count and clinical chemistry parameter changes at doses of 12 mg or above. Non-adverse 
9 
changes in histopathology and clinical pathology were observed in multiple organs at all dose levels. All 
10 
were reversible except for macrophage infiltration in the brain and optic nerve, microvesicular neuronal 
11 
vacuolation in the spinal cord, and minimal increases in kidney weight. Based on the general reversibility 
12 
and non-severity of these changes, and the lack of any correlation with clinical signs or clinical pathology 
13 
findings, BIIB094 40 mg was established as the no observable adverse event level (NOAEL).  
14 
Pharmacokinetics 
15 
BIIB094 reached peak concentration (Cmax) in serum 1–4 hours after administration in macaques 
16 
(Supplementary Fig. 5A and 5B). Serum levels then declined biphasically and fell to close to, or below, the 
17 
lower limit of quantification (LLOQ; 1.00 ng/mL) by 48 hours after administration. Geometric mean Cmax and 
18 
area under the curve (AUC) at the NOAEL of BIIB094 40 mg were 22,000 ng/mL and 134,000 hr*ng/mL, 
19 
respectively. In animals receiving BIIB094 40 mg, mean BIIB094 levels reached approximately 60 µg/g in 
20 
the frontal cortex, 1400 µg/g in the kidney cortex and 3 µg/g in the lung (Supplementary Fig. 5C–E). 
21 
Pharmacodynamics  
22 
Levels of LRRK2 mRNA were significantly lowered at all doses studied in the frontal cortex of macaques 
23 
compared with artificial CSF (aCSF) control (> 90%; p < 0.0001), but not in the kidney cortex or lung 
24 
(Supplementary Fig. 5F–H).

Methods: Preclinical study in cynomolgus macaques 
27 
Animals and ethics 
28 
The study in cynomolgus macaques (Macaca fascicularis) was performed by Charles River Laboratories 
29 
(Montreal ULC). Animals (N = 32) were 2–3 years old at experimentation start, of mixed sex (4 males, 4 
30 
females per cohort) and of Asian origin. The study was performed in compliance with Good Laboratory 
31 
Practice and as accepted by Regulatory Authorities throughout the European Union, United States of 
32 
America (Food and Drug Administration), Japan (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare), and other 
33 


===PAGE 3===
countries that are signatories to the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) 
34 
Mutual Acceptance of Data Agreement. 
35 
Dosing 
36 
Animals were food deprived at least 6 hours prior to dosing. Animals were anesthetized. Percutaneous 
37 
lumbar IT dosing of BIIB094 12, 24 or 40 mg formulated in 1 mL aCSF was performed on days 1, 29, 57, 
38 
85, 113, 141, 169, 197, 225, 253 and 281. Control animals received aCSF. Injections were performed using 
39 
atraumatic spinal needles (e.g., B Braun, 25 Gauge, 1 inch) and were followed by a 0.25 mL aCSF flush. 
40 
Animals were anesthetized with ketamine/medetomidine anesthesia, dosed in a lateral recumbency 
41 
position and remained in a prone position for 15 minutes after dosing was complete, before administration 
42 
of atipamezole reversal agent.  
43 
Tissue dissection for RNA extraction and real-time quantitative reverse transcription polymerase chain 
44 
reaction (qRT-PCR) 
45 
Animals were sacrificed 7 days after the last dose. Brain slices, kidney cortex and left lung lobes were 
46 
collected, flash frozen on dry ice and stored frozen (–60°C to –90°C). At the time of sampling, 2 mm biopsy 
47 
punches were used to collect frontal cortex from frozen brain slices and kidney cortex and lung samples for 
48 
RNA extraction. Each sample was homogenized in 1 mL of Trizol reagent (Thermofisher Scientific, 
49 
Waltham, MA) and total RNA was extracted according to the manufacturer’s protocol. Total RNA was 
50 
purified further using a mini-RNA purification kit (Qiagen, Valencia, CA). After purification, the RNA samples 
51 
were subjected to real-time qRT-PCR analysis using the Life Technologies ABI QuantStudio 7 Flex 
52 
Sequence Detection System (Applied Biosystems Inc, Carlsbad CA). Briefly, 10 µL RT-PCRs containing 
53 
400 nL of RNA were run with the AgPath- ID One-Step qRT-PCR Kit (Thermofisher Scientific, Waltham, 
54 
MA) reagents with primer probe sets targeting monkey LRRK2 and PPIA, a ubiquitously expressed 
55 
housekeeping gene. Primers and probes were ordered from IDT Technologies. Primers were as follows: 
56 
LRRK2 forward primer, AGATGATTCACTCAGGTCATCAA; LRRK2 reverse primer, 
57 
CTCTCAGAAGCCAGAGAAGAAA; LRRK2 probe, TCCCATATGAGGCATTCAGACAGCA with 5’FAM and 
58 
3’MGB chemistry; PPIA forward primer, TGCTGGACCCAACACAAATG; PPIA reverse primer, 
59 
TGCCATCCAACCACTCAGTC; PPIA probe’ TTCCCAGTTTTTCATCTGCACTGCCA with 5’FAM and 3’ 
60 
TAMRA chemistry. All qPCRs were run in technical triplicates; outliers resulting in a greater than 20% 
61 
coefficient of variation from sample average were excluded. The expression level of total LRRK2 mRNA 
62 
was normalized to that of PPIA mRNA, and this was further normalized to that measured in control animals 
63 
that received aCSF. LRRK2 mRNA levels were reported as a percentage of aCSF control. Data were 
64 
analyzed using Microsoft Excel. One-way ANOVA with Dunnett’s multiple comparison against aCSF-
65 
treated group were used to analyze mRNA data from macaques treated with aCSF or BIIB094 (GraphPad 
66 
Prism 10.4.2).

Supplementary methods: REASON study 
76 
Antisense oligonucleotide synthesis  
77 
The synthesis of oligonucleotides was carried out on an AKTA Process solid-phase synthesizer using the 
78 
NittoPhase HL Unylinker (Kinovate Life Sciences) as the solid support at ambient temperature. The 
79 
phosphoramidite starting materials (MOE-A, MOE-G, MOE-5MeC, MOE-5MeU, Deoxy-A, Deoxy-G, deoxy-T 
80 
and deoxy-5MeC) were purchased from Hongene. Solvents and reagents were procured from Tedia 
81 
Company, LLC. 
82 
The 4-reaction synthesis for attaching each nucleotide consisted of detritylation, coupling, capping and 
83 
sulfurization or oxidation reactions. The detritylation step was carried out using a 10% solution of 
84 
dichloroacetic acid in toluene as the reagent. The coupling step was carried out by delivering equal 
85 
volumes of a solution of the corresponding phosphoramidite (0.2 M in acetonitrile) and a solution of 4,5-
86 
dicyanoimidazole (1.0 M in acetonitrile containing 0.1 M N-methylimidazole). The sulfurization step was 
87 
completed by delivery of 0.2 M of PADS (Phenylacetyl disulfide) in acetonitrile/3-picoline (1:1, v/v) (the 
88 
PADS solution was aged for at least 12 h before use according to the literature procedure)1. The oxidation 
89 
was performed by delivering I2 solution (0.05 M in pyridine-H2O, 9:1, v/v) to the column. The capping step 
90 
is performed after the sulfurization or oxidation steps. The capping step was performed by delivery of the 
91 
same amount of a solution of N-methyl imidazole−pyridine−ACN (2:3:5, v/v/v) and a solution of acetic 
92 
anhydride in ACN (1:4, v/v) to the solid support. After each reaction, the solid support was washed with 
93 
acetonitrile. After the synthesis was completed, the phosphorus protecting group was removed by 
94 
circulating a mixture of Triethylamine and acetonitrile (1:1, v/v). Cleavage of the oligonucleotides from the 
95 


===PAGE 5===
support and base deprotection were performed in ∼30% NH4OH at 50−60°C in a stainless-steel vessel to 
96 
afford the crude DMT-on oligonucleotide product.  
97 
The crude DMT-on intermediate is purified by reversed-phase high performance liquid chromatography 
98 
(RP-HPLC) using a polymer stationary phase and aqueous sodium acetate/methanol mobile phases. The 
99 
DMT-on product peak is collected, and the other peaks are discarded.  
100 
The Trityl eluate obtained is precipitated with ethanol, isolated by sedimentation and decantation, and 
101 
reconstituted in purified water to reduce levels of methanol and sodium acetate. Reconstituted 5′-O-DMT-
102 
on material is detritylated by addition of glacial acetic acid for 5-10 h and the reaction is quenched with 5 N 
103 
sodium hydroxide (NaOH). The resulting detritylated material is precipitated and reconstituted two more 
104 
times. Finally, the solution of purified oligonucleotide is transferred into freeze drying trays and placed in a 
105 
freeze dryer. The material is frozen and then dried under reduced pressure to afford the amorphous Drug 
106 
Substance.

Protein preparation and digestion for liquid chromatography mass spectrometry (LC-MS) analysis for 
108 
biomarker detection  
109 
Equal 500 µL volumes of each CSF sample were denatured with 1X RIPA lysis buffer (Millipore Sigma 20-
110 
188). Samples were then spiked with 2 pg/mL of LRRK2 stable isotope labeled peptide K13C(6)15N(2) 
111 
AEEGDLLVNPDQPR13C(6)15N(2) and 2 pg/mL of pRab10 T72 stable isotope labeled peptide 
112 
FHTITTSYYR13C(6)15N(2) as internal standards. Samples were then digested for 1.5 hours at 37°C with 10 µg 
113 
of TPCK treated trypsin (Millipore Sigma T1426). Standard curve samples were prepared by adding LRRK2 
114 
recombinant protein (Thermo Fisher A15197) and pRab10 T72 recombinant protein (kindly provided by 
115 
Dario Alessi, University of Dundee) to 500 µL of 0.1% bovine serum albumin surrogate matrix followed by 
116 
addition of 1X RIPA lysis buffer and internal standards. Standard curve intervals were constructed at 1, 2, 
117 
5, 10, 25, 50, 75, 100 pg/mL for LRRK2 and 0.2, 0.4, 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 20 pg/mL for pRab10.  
118 
Immunoprecipitation of LRRK2 and pRab10 target peptides  
119 
Immunoprecipitation of LRRK2 and pRab10 target peptides was performed on an Agilent AssayMAP 
120 
Bravo. First, N241A/34 LRRK2 antibody (Antibodies Inc. 75-253) and MJF-R20 pRab8A phospho T72 
121 
antibody (Abcam ab230260) were labeled with ChromaLINK biotin (Vector Labs B-1001). Antibodies were 
122 
then immobilized on AssayMAP 5 µL streptavidin SA-W cartridges (Agilent Technologies G5496-60010) at 
123 
a ratio of 1 µg of each antibody per cartridge. Digested sample was then loaded onto each cartridge at a 
124 
flow rate of 10 µL/min followed by a PBS wash. Samples were then eluted with 50 µL of 2% acetonitrile in 
125 
water acidified with 0.1% formic acid.

Eluted samples were loaded onto an Evotip Pure and chromatographically separated using an Evosep One 
128 
running the 40SPD method and coupled to a Thermo EASY-Spray PepMap RSLC C18 column (Thermo 
129
