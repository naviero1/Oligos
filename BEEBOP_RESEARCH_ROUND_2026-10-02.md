Exact sequence is necessary for construct linkage but insufficient alone. Sugar/base/linkage position, strand identity, conjugates, stereochemistry and formulation can distinguish molecules sharing a base sequence. Reference identity is not experimental-batch identity. Shared sequence/family grouping for leakage prevention must not merge chemically distinct administered constructs.
