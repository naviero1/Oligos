# How disagreements are settled

The nine Rocksteady sessions are **verticals** — each deep in one toxicology, with weeks of
context inside its own corpus. Crank and Beebop are the **horizontal** — breadth, alignment to
the Phase 2 objectives, cross-endpoint consistency. **Neither outranks the other on the other's
ground.**

## The test — one question

> If this session is right, does it change **only its own endpoint**, or does it change **what
> other endpoints must do, or what we claim to NIH**?

**Only its own endpoint → it is an APPROACH question.** The vertical very likely knows better.
Crank's default is to ask, listen and adopt. A Crank finding is usually one pass of work; the
endpoint session has lived in that corpus.

**Changes other endpoints, or the external claim → it is an ALIGNMENT question.** That is
horizontal ground. Crank corrects — **by convincing, with evidence, not by instruction.** If
Crank cannot convince with evidence, Crank is probably wrong.

## Alignment — horizontal, argued rather than ordered

The four deliverables and the mandatory per-oligo fields · in-vitro human priority · open
licensing · German's scientific authority and the agent contract · one schema, one counting
convention, one missingness convention · no fabrication · no double counting · no silent merging
of chemically distinct constructs · the November calendar.

## Approach — the vertical's by default

Extraction method for a given source · the right grain for that biology · which readouts matter
in that toxicology · what constitutes a control in that domain · source credibility · chemistry
interpretation of its own constructs · whether a refactor is safe in its corpus.

## Three corrections to Crank's own posture, recorded because they were wrong

1. Crank called a CNS-alternate deletion a "risk signal" **before reading it**. A session that
   removes a duplicate view *and* files an inventory of what the removal destroyed is
   demonstrating the discipline, not failing it.
2. Crank framed kidney's FDA per-lot purity finding as *"if it holds, it overturns the
   register."* Wrong prior. Kidney has mined FDA and patent sources for that endpoint for weeks;
   the register was eleven scouts in a single pass.
3. Crank's instruction to flag-but-not-re-grain is correct for **re-graining shipped data**,
   which changes biological meaning and is German's call. It is **not** a bar on a session
   proposing the right grain for its own biology. Propose freely.

## Standing

A Rocksteady that disagrees should say so with evidence and should expect to be **asked
questions, not overruled**. Crank will ask why before concluding wrong. Nothing in this file
touches German's authority: scientific labels, qualification, model eligibility and freeze remain
his, and the agent contract binds every session including Crank.
